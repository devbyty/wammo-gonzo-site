The Wammo Gonzo Questline

An RPG-style way to listen to the show and explore the city like it's a video game.

Complete these quests in any order. Send proof to the show if you want a shoutout or a shot at Player of the Week.

  1. Main Quest: Listen to any episode and say out loud: “I accept the Wammo Gonzo Quest.”
  2. Brewery Mood Meter: Visit a brewery, vibe-check the room, and secretly rate it 1–5.
  3. Spot the Fake Mayor: Identify someone in public with “mayor energy.”
  4. Bike Trail Ghost: On a trail, say: “If the ghost shows, I'm prepared.”
  5. Public Podcast Mention: Casually mention Wammo Gonzo in a conversation where someone overhears.

You can add or rotate quests over time. Treat this like patch notes for your own IRL game.
